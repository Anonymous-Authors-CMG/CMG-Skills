## Generate a message

1. Stage the changes you want to commit.
2. Click **✨** in the Source Control title bar.

Within half a minute you get:

- the open issue this change most likely addresses, with the reason, pre-selected when the match is strong;
- a draft that says what changed **and why**, ending with `Refs #12`.

Edit it, ask for a revision ("shorter", "mention the perf impact"), or pick another issue, then **Use this message**. It goes into the commit box. Nothing is staged, committed or closed for you.

Tip: name your branch after the issue (`fix/123-crash`) and that issue is always considered.
